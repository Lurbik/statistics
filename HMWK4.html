The thread connecting the two exercises

δ = x − x̄  →  x̄ += δ / n  →  SS += δ (x − x̄)      S² = SS / (n − 1)

Exercise 1 proves that these three lines give the exact mean and sum of squares after every new observation. In Exercise 2 each hacker runs them once per attacked system, so the sampling mean and sampling variance are available after every system without storing the trajectory. Averaging these per-hacker results over the m hackers shows that E[X̄] = μ and E[S²] = σ² for every n ≥ 2. Var(X̄) = σ²/n decreases exactly like 1/n; Var(S²) has an exact formula (implemented on the page) that, for the distributions used, decreases asymptotically like 1/n; the corresponding standard deviations decrease like 1/√n.

Builds on HMWK 3 (Bernoulli and Rademacher penetration scores): the Binomial(10, p) score option in Ex. 2 is the sum of 10 Bernoulli increments.

How to read and check the pages

  • Every number shown is computed when the page opens; nothing is typed in by hand. Each page runs its own self-tests (bottom section) that compare the formulas with independent methods, including exact enumeration.
